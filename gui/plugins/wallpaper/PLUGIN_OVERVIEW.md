Put your own image or GIF behind the New thread screen and beside your chats.

## What you get

- Six slots. Add a JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF to each and switch with one click.
- Per-wallpaper settings: visibility, fill or fit, and a pixel effect for still images.
- Half wallpaper on New thread: the image fades out halfway down. Chats show it beside the messages, never behind the text.

## How it works

Open **Settings → Appearance → Wallpaper** and pick a slot. Big photos are shrunk to 2560 px before they are saved. Images stay on this computer, and every open window updates right away.

## Requirements

None. Files can be up to 8 MB. The pixel effect does not apply to GIFs.
